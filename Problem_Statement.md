# Business Problem Statement: Enterprise LLM Benchmarking & Risk Mitigation

## 1. Background & Context
As enterprises aggressively transition Large Language Models (LLMs) from experimental sandboxes to user-facing applications, they face an unprecedented software challenge: **stochastic output variability**. Unlike deterministic code, LLMs are prone to hallucinations, behavioral deviations, systemic bias alignment shifts, and vulnerability to social engineering or authority bias. 

For businesses operating in highly compliant markets like Ireland (under EU AI Act regulations) and the GCC region, deploying unmonitored AI architectures poses massive compliance, legal, and operational risks. 

## 2. The Core Business Challenge
> *"How can an enterprise quantitatively benchmark LLM response vectors, audit moral boundaries, track sycophancy patterns and establish measurable, automated guardrails to eliminate hallucination risks before deploying models into production?"*

## 3. Project Objectives
1. **Develop an Automated Benchmark Pipeline:** Move past qualitative "vibe-based" prompt testing and establish deterministic programmatic metrics.
2. **Audit Alignment & Sycophancy Profiles:** Analyze how models shift baseline logic when pressured with contradictory assertions or authoritative inputs.
3. **Establish Ethical Guardrails:** Track ethical alignment, systemic biases, and negative constraint adherence across multi-turn workflows.

## 4. Measurable Deliverables
* **Synthetic Evaluation Dataset:** A target test suite containing structured prompts designed to stress-test constraint compliance, data factual grounding, and logical stubbornness.
* **Programmatic Evaluation Pipeline:** A production-ready Python script simulating multi-turn user interventions and computing statistical performance indexes.
* **Strategic AI Governance Playbook:** Comprehensive business recommendations detailing operational guidelines (The 4Ds Matrix: Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence) for corporate data offices.
